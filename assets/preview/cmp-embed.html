Questions people ask

What is the difference between the Advanced, Ultimate and Signature blood tests?

Advanced (93 biomarkers, £269) is our essential comprehensive test: full blood count, cholesterol with ApoB and ApoA-1, liver, kidney, glucose and HbA1c, iron and vitamins, full thyroid with TPO and thyroglobulin antibodies, full hormones (testosterone, SHBG, LH, FSH, oestradiol, progesterone, prolactin, cortisol, DHEA-S), PhenoAge and TrueScore, from a blood sample only. Ultimate (140 biomarkers for men, 139 for women, £399) adds PSA for men, Lp(a) with the cardiovascular risk score, fasting insulin and C-peptide, cystatin C, PTH, tumour markers, immunoglobulins, coeliac screening, urine analysis with culture and the No findings, no fee guarantee. Signature (230 biomarkers, £799) is processed by Randox Health and adds specialist markers such as homocysteine, ApoE, IGF-1, cardiac injury markers, ANA, anti-CCP, extra tumour markers and physical measurements.

Which panel should I choose for HRT or menopause?

Both measure the core menopause and HRT markers: FSH, LH, oestradiol, progesterone, prolactin, testosterone and SHBG, plus thyroid antibodies. Advanced suits regular HRT monitoring. Ultimate adds Lp(a), fasting insulin and cancer markers, so it is the better first full test.

Does Advanced include Lp(a) and fasting insulin?

No. Lp(a), fasting insulin, C-peptide and HOMA-IR are in Ultimate and Signature only. Advanced includes ApoB, ApoA-1 and the ApoB/A-1 ratio, glucose and HbA1c.

Is everything in Ultimate also in Signature?

Almost. Signature adds around 90 extra markers, but because it runs on Randox Health's laboratory rather than our UKAS partner laboratory, a few Ultimate markers are not on it: free PSA and the PSA ratio, RDW, MPV, total protein, and urine culture with sensitivities. If free PSA matters to you, choose Ultimate or ask us about adding it to Signature.

Can I check whether a specific test is included?

Yes. Paste or type the tests you have been asked to have into the checker on this page. It shows which panel covers all of them, and lists anything missing from each panel.

Are some markers only tested in men or women?

Yes. PSA is tested for men; CA-125 and CA 15-3 for women. That is why Ultimate is 140 biomarkers for men and 139 for women.

What does 'calc' mean?

Calculated. Some results are clinical indices worked out from measured values, such as HOMA-IR from glucose and insulin, or FIB-4 from liver enzymes and platelets. They are listed so you can see exactly what your report will show.
